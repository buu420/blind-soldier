# Blind Soldier 0.9.9

Adds native accessibility support for Postscriptthree's Gameplay Tweaks 1.78
through 7th Heaven, including setups with Echo-S 7.

The second accessory row is announced in Equip and Status. Equipment comparison
follows the stats actually displayed by the patched screen, and multi-linked
Materia sockets follow their native groups. Battle PHS and the revised Limit
menu now announce their actual selected entries and availability.
Materia reward IDs retain the complete results readout, including experience,
AP, gil and the actual loaded Materia name.

Item, ability, equipment and Materia text follows the native loader's current
data, including FFNx 1.24.3 overrides. Binding tolerates either startup order and
waits for the loader to own its getter. Postscript's displayed HP help,
effectiveness labels and steal/morph labels are spoken after their native draws.
Undisplayed enemy information is not queried.

Character Order Randomizer guidance follows the actual remapped party roles;
fixed field models and movie descriptions retain their visual identities.
Randomized field pickups remain navigable as “Item pickup” or “Materia pickup”
until the game reveals the reward. The modified opening field has checked
navigation and description cue positions.

All 42 actionable Postscript options were audited. See the
[compatibility guide](../compatibility/postscript-1.78.md) for the verified versions,
option coverage and upstream restrictions. Postscript's 7th Heaven patches run
on the legacy edition; the native Steam edition retains stock behavior and the
shared accessibility fixes.

See [validation](../validation/postscript-20261013.md) for native research and
test boundaries. Offline regression and package checks do not establish live
speech, controller behavior or a complete playthrough of randomized seeds.

Close the game before updating. Saves, player settings, narration history and
existing audio-description recordings are preserved. This release adds no
recordings and uses the existing audio-description controls.
