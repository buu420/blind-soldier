# Blind Soldier 0.2.2 (Beta)

This beta release adds route-driven automatic walking and repairs the Train
Graveyard story route while keeping the public version number free of numbered
beta suffixes.

## What changed

- Adds `P` as the auto-walk toggle after selecting a field or world-map target
  with the normal navigation controls.
- Uses the same native walkmesh route, route corrections, arrival handling, and
  progress indicator as manual navigation.
- Releases movement during menus, dialogue, battles, movies, loading, focus
  loss, unreadable state, route failure, and arrival.
- Reasserts the current movement direction when FFVII stops reporting an owned
  auto-walk input, preventing a route from silently stalling until `P` is
  toggled off and on.
- Tracks the Train Graveyard's native train positions, including the recovery
  state after reversing the first train, the upper train, and the actual Sector
  7 Station exit.
- Routes around the first train's reverse trigger while approaching the upper
  train and follows the live walkmesh after each train moves.
- Applies the same shared auto-walk and navigation behavior to the legacy x86
  and Steam 2026 x64 backends.

## Testing requested

Select a field or world-map destination, press `P`, and confirm movement follows
the spoken route while the normal progress indicator advances. In the Train
Graveyard, confirm Story advances through both train controls and then reaches
the upper exit to Sector 7 Station.

Auto walk never presses the action button. Interactions, train controls,
ladders before mounting, and exits that require confirmation remain under the
player's control.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching beta-channel update directly.
