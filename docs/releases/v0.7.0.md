# Blind Soldier 0.7.0

This prerelease fixes the reported Wutai Pagoda, North Corel approach, post-date Cait Sith chase, and Temple of the Ancients clock issues for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- **Wutai Pagoda:** Story follows all five opponents and floors using the game's current floor, victories and party state. Available stairs appear in Exits with their floor names, and auto walk stops after changing floors. The save point is outside in Wutai's main street.
- **North Corel approach:** world auto walk avoids the Gold Saucer desert boundary that can push a walking party back and interrupt movement. Visible world dialogue is read aloud; auto walk releases movement while the game or dialogue owns control and retains the selected destination.
- **Cait Sith chase:** Story guidance follows the chase after the Gold Saucer date through the squares and ticket office, with routes back from other squares.
- **Temple clock:** both runtimes read the visible hands, open bridges and the player's doorway or middle platform. Press `R` to repeat the current activity. Story offers doorway six when the necessary bridges connect the player's platform; otherwise it explains the native clock controls. The moving second hand no longer appears as a static exit.

The field regressions check 2,028 clock states against the installed walkmesh and native bridge locks. Twenty-five movement replays cover the logged North Corel approach from five positions under five camera angles. Story and transit checks cover both runtime assemblies and both installed field archives, and the complete runtime suites passed against the packaged DLLs before release preparation.

Live gameplay testing is still needed. These checks do not certify a complete playthrough of the Pagoda battles, chase or clock puzzle. Avoiding the clock's moving second hand still depends on the player's timing. The [investigation report](https://github.com/buu420/blind-soldier/blob/v0.7.0/docs/research/2026-09-25-pagoda-clock-world-navigation.md) records the evidence and scope.
