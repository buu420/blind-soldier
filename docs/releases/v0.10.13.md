# Blind Soldier 0.10.13 beta

- The battle results screen reads visible Materia level-ups and learned Limit Breaks.
- Chocobo Ranch feeding and breeding menus read the highlighted item, its displayed count, and the feeding quantity as it changes. The newborn’s color is announced when it appears. Visible stable birds include their color in NPC navigation.
- Navigation guides you into interaction range of Tifa’s desk in Nibelheim and checks that Cloud is facing it. Confirm opens the report, and speech reads its full visible text.
- Junon send-off commands are announced as soon as the command window finishes drawing.
- In-progress scene descriptions can finish across supported transitions before the next screen speaks.

Close the game before updating. This beta covers the 2013 and Steam 2026 versions of Final Fantasy VII.
