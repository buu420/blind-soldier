# Blind Soldier 0.5.10

This prerelease fixes the screen reader repeating dialogue over FFNx voice acting after loading a save directly into an area such as Wall Market or Honeybee Manor.

Voice suppression now uses confirmed FFNx playback immediately. Previously it stayed inactive until the player entered an area with a recognized audio-description script. Dialogue without a voice recording and dialogue choices remain readable. Voice playback results are now logged in every field to make future reports easier to diagnose.

The regression reproduced duplicate speech before the fix and passes afterward. Checks cover missing voice files, unavailable hooks, unvoiced later pages, choice fallback, and unrelated windows. The existing native-hook verification remains required; unsupported FFNx builds retain screen-reader dialogue. In-game confirmation from the reporter is still pending.

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** both supported game runtimes.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy 2013/7th Heaven x86.
- **ffviinew-v0.5.10-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.5.10-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

Each archive has a SHA-256 sidecar. Preserve `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration\config.json` when updating a portable installation. Restart the game after updating.
