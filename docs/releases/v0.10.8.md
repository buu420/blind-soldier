# Blind Soldier 0.10.8 beta

Repairs Ancient Forest navigation, Emerald selection and Bone Village's excavation pickup on both runtimes.

- **Ancient Forest:** available in Locations through the game's model-contact entrance. After Ultimate Weapon opens the basin, navigation can find a grass landing for the Highwind and continue on foot. Black and gold chocobos can use their native terrain access earlier; dismount when prompted. At the entrance, press your normal Confirm button to enter.
- **Emerald Weapon:** stays in underwater Events while its native entity exists. Before the first sighting, the row says its location is unknown. A positive sighting supplies its current position; leaving view retains a clearly labelled last-seen location so the menu can still be used. Navigation to that remembered area uses normal submarine travel depth and stops there. Positive resightings update the route. Hidden patrol coordinates are never used, and ordinary submarine routes retain Emerald avoidance.
- **Bone Village:** look in Objects for **Excavation results chest** after digging. It now has the chest cue and the native Talk approach, including facing the chest during auto walk. Press Confirm to open it and receive the excavation result, including the Key to Sector 5 when the game awards it. The reward is revealed by the game when opened.

Close the game before updating. Saves, settings, description histories, existing recordings and normal interaction controls are preserved. Native-data and automated checks cover both runtimes and five Echo-S layouts. These checks are separate from live gameplay, physical-controller and audible screen-reader testing, which were not performed for this repair.
