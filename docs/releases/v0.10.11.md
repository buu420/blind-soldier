# Blind Soldier 0.10.11 beta

## Bone Village

Story lists the Key to Sector 5 errand when the game can award the key, including
on older saves. Talk to the excavation foreman and choose **Normal Treasure**.
In **Objects**, select **Dig spot: Key to Sector 5**. It appears before the blast
and guides you to a valid dig point even if the workers point to an empty spot.

When the game asks where to dig, press **Switch** at the spot. Back in Bone
Village, use Story to find the results chest and press **Confirm** to open it.
Collecting the key removes its listing.

## Ancient Forest

In **Objects**, each insect and frog's number is followed by a location cue
that updates when the creature is moved. Opening Objects reads the pitcher
plants from left to right and says which are open, closed, or shaking. Select
individual throwing spots as they become available.

Close the game before updating. This beta covers the 2013 x86 and Steam 2026
x64 versions of Final Fantasy VII.
