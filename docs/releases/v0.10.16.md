# Blind Soldier 0.10.16 beta

- On 7th Heaven with FFNx 1.25.2, Blind Soldier reads the selected Materia and shop items from the live game text. This restores speech for entries such as Ice, Iron Bangle, Assault Gun, and Grenade in Echo-S 7.
- The unopened Phoenix Down chest in Mako Reactor 1 and the missing Northern Crater chest are selectable in **Objects**. Each stays selectable as **Chest** when its item name is unavailable. Open it with Confirm.
- Ancient Forest gives shorter puzzle updates and warns once when the plant under you starts shaking. **Objects** describes the selected target and **Story** gives the next step. For the full puzzle status, press **R** or hold one trigger and press **Y/Triangle**.

This beta is available for the 2013 x86 game, including 7th Heaven, and Steam 2026 x64. Close the game before updating. On 7th Heaven, close the manager as well.
