# Blind Soldier 0.10.4

This testing release repairs Emerald approaches and Sector 8 underground ladder exits. Close the game before updating.

- **Emerald Weapon:** a positive sighting tells you to select it in **Events** and prepares it as your next Events preview while you browse another category. Underwater Events says each item's position and total count. Pursue follows the target you heard; if it disappears, navigation asks you to choose another instead of silently changing destinations. Emerald uses the game's current visible state, including early submarine access.
- **Emerald approaches:** navigation can approach a visible Emerald floating above a seabed surface the submarine cannot cross, using nearby traversable water inside its native contact range. The target still reports the observed creature's position. Loss of sight stops pursuit; ordinary submarine routes retain their avoidance behavior.
- **Sector 8 underground:** exits that require a ladder now include the climb in their route. Navigation announces the normal mount button and climbing direction and waits for your action before continuing. This covers both scaffold fields and the available Echo-S layouts.

Both legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam x64 are included. Normal interaction and encounter controls remain in effect. Saves, settings, description histories and recordings are preserved.

Automated checks cover native route and arrival behavior, visible/hidden targets, early and later progress, ladder action holds, and five Echo-S variants. Live traversal, physical controller use and audible speech for this repair remain untested. See the [validation details](../validation/emerald-midgar-20261023.md).
