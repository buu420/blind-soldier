# Blind Soldier 0.10.19 beta

- Ancient Forest's second screen now starts its insect, vine, frog and beehive progression on entry. Story checks which side of the final flytrap you are on before giving the exit or reset instruction.
- For live testing, start Story on entering screen two, follow the vine and frog launches, collect Slash-All, then continue toward the treetops and cave treasures. The full timed puzzle and the reported initial Auto Walk delay still need in-game testing.

This beta is available for Steam 2026 x64 and FFVII 2013 x86, including 7th Heaven. Close the game before updating. If you use 7th Heaven, close it as well.
