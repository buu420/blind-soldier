# Blind Soldier 0.8.10

This beta adds held-trigger controller controls and spoken mod settings to
legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64. Close the game before updating.

- Hold either trigger to browse navigation. D-pad Left/Right changes category;
  Up/Down changes target. L3 starts guidance, R3 starts automatic travel, and
  B/Circle stops. Release the trigger before browsing again after a start or stop.
- In battle, hold LT/L2 for party readouts. Left/Right selects a present member,
  Up reads HP, MP and limit, Down reads statuses, and Y/Triangle reads limit.
- Press F11 or both triggers plus Y/Triangle for mod settings. Keyboard controls
  are J/L for settings, U/O for values, I to activate, K to repeat, and F11 to close.
  Controller controls are Up/Down, Left/Right, A/Cross, and B/Circle. The page stays
  open after releasing the triggers and stays audible with Mod speech off.
- Description volume is the first setting, with saved levels from 50 to 300 percent.
  It applies to current and future recorded descriptions. Screen-reader volume is
  separate, and a limiter controls boosted peaks.
- Reset battle descriptions is the second setting. Activate it twice to reset
  the current save, so the next occurrence of each summon or limit is described
  again. Playing recordings finish normally; other saves and room history are kept.
- Player toggles explain restart requirements and runtime support. F5-F8 shortcuts
  save their choices too. Updates preserve player overrides and description history.

R3 is reserved by controller capture, including outside a trigger chord, to prevent
the Steam Battle Assist release shortcut from firing in either button order.
Battle Assist remains available in the game's Boosts menu. Navigation, settings
and party chords consume native buttons and movement axes until release.

Automated checks cover both hosts, native state readouts, input ownership and
release tails, save-bound history reset, persistence, and actual recording samples
through the playback gain and limiter. An isolated SDL virtual-controller test
also exercises real native button and axis hooks. Live gameplay and hardware
controller delivery remain to be verified by players.
