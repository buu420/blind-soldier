# Blind Soldier 0.6.5

This prerelease restores missing Story guidance and improves navigation for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- Restores Rocket Town's first-visit Story objective and removes a misleading backyard-door objective.
- Keeps auto walk climbing the rocket ladder through brief tracking interruptions and improves routes around NPCs in Cid's house.
- Adds missing later-story connections and return steps from side rooms, including the northern journey, Highwind, Huge Materia, underwater reactor, Temple, and later Cosmo Canyon visits.
- Corrects the Highwind destination after Bugenhagen's return and adds reachable Story steps for the Midgar wall-climb return route.
- Prevents moving NPCs from making the entire x64 NPC list disappear when the scene's identity and activation state remain stable.
- Makes spoken directions clearer when the route begins with a very short turn.

Both full regression suites passed against the packaged mod DLLs. The Story audit checked 258 progression checkpoints for each of four runtime/archive combinations. These checks cover native scripts, availability and route geometry; a complete live playthrough remains unverified. Please report remaining missing Story targets, NPCs or stuck routes with a recent log.

Existing movie and cutscene narration is preserved. This release adds no new recordings.

## Downloads

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** both supported game runtimes.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy 2013/7th Heaven x86.
- **ffviinew-v0.6.5-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.6.5-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

Each archive has a SHA-256 sidecar. Preserve the entire Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration folder when updating a portable installation, including room-descriptions.json. Restart the game after updating.
