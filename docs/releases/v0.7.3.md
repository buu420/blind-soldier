# Blind Soldier 0.7.3

This prerelease fixes the Temple of the Ancients doorway chase and adds Bone Village excavation feedback for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- **Temple doorway chase:** all nine tunnel entrances and both jump ledges appear in Exits, labelled by floor and position. Routes now approach the entrance on its actual floor instead of stopping on an overlapping floor. The optional ledges no longer block navigation elsewhere in the room.
- **Visible Ancient movements:** brief cues announce where the Ancient appears, enters a tunnel or drops to another floor. Player tunnel arrivals are also announced. R repeats the current details; it does not reveal the Ancient's next destination.
- **Bone Village targets:** the two ladders and visible diggers away from their waiting positions appear in Objects. Ladder targets tell you when to use OK/Confirm. Workers are announced as placed only after their native placement sequence finishes and they settle.
- **Digging feedback:** brief cues identify excavation stages, worker placement, the blast and searching. After the blast, feedback describes the workers' visible sightlines. R gives the current stage and worker positions, floors and facing directions. A failed read no longer creates a false sightline change when reading recovers.

Hiring, placing workers, starting the blast and selecting a digging spot still use the game's controls. Hidden buried-item positions are not read. A worker placed exactly at their original waiting position cannot be distinguished reliably from a waiting worker, so the mod does not claim that worker has been placed.

## Testing

Both complete runtime suites and the shared and parity suites passed against the exact packaged DLLs. Additional native-data checks cover every walkmesh triangle to each of the nine Temple entrances and real navigation starts for the new targets. The release also passed package, native dependency and Ghidra verification.

Live testing is still needed: neither puzzle has been played through with this build. Please report whether every Temple entrance can be reached and whether Bone Village's placement, blast and digging stages remain understandable through the whole excavation.
