# Blind Soldier 0.9.6

Northern Crater Story guidance now follows the ledge you are standing on. It
guides you through the side caves and the climb needed to return to the onward
route, instead of offering jumps on other ledges or circling between two rooms.
The normal Exits category still lists the other available doors.

Navigation remains available during the playable rock-platform sections before
Jenova. Those rooms use looping background movies; the mod now distinguishes
them from cutscenes while respecting the game's control locks and dialogue.

This applies to the 2013/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 runtimes. Climbing with
Confirm, dialogue, party formation and encounters use your normal game buttons.

Native-data route and input-ownership regressions passed for both runtimes.
Live endgame gameplay, controller response and screen-reader listening still
need player confirmation. See [validation](../validation/northern-crater-20261012.md).

Close the game before updating. Existing saves, settings and description history
are preserved.
