# Blind Soldier 0.8.9

This beta repairs Temple of the Ancients clock warnings and miniature-temple
navigation on legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64. Close the game before updating.

- While crossing a clock-hand bridge, speech names the visible second hand and
  warns that it can knock you off. Its observed approach, crossing and passage
  are announced automatically. Approaching and crossing warnings can interrupt
  an older reading so they reach you while they are current.
- Repeat retains the readable second-hand warning even if another clock hand
  cannot be read. Warnings respect speech settings, game focus and the native
  hazard's enabled state.
- After the Red Dragon, Story names "Press Confirm at the miniature temple at
  the far right of the hall". Objects lists "Miniature temple; press Confirm at
  it". Both guide you to the game's interaction line in front of the model and
  announce arrival at the native touch distance.

Use the usual R3 navigation menu to select the miniature temple in Story or
Objects. Avoiding the second hand and pressing Confirm at the temple remain
under your control.

Installed script and walkmesh checks, read-only Ghidra analysis and runtime
regressions cover the native hazard gate, all twelve clock bridges, current
speech delivery and the four post-boss statue routes. Live gameplay and actual
keyboard/controller hook delivery still need player feedback.
