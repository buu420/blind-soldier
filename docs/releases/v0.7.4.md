# Blind Soldier 0.7.4

This prerelease adds a Bone Village dig-spot shortcut and repairs reproduced autowalk stalls in legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- **Bone Village dig spot:** Objects now includes one "Dig spot: [treasure]" target for the current search. The Lunar Harp spot is available from arrival; good and normal treasure spots appear after the blast, when the game chooses their destination. The shortcut intentionally reveals the buried destination, making the visual excavation activity easier to complete.
- **Reaching the treasure:** spoken navigation and auto walk use the spot's precise digging area, including the ladder when needed. Arrival requires standing on the ground triangle the game checks, so proximity alone cannot stop navigation short of the spot. Use OK at the ladder and Switch to place a digger or confirm the final digging point. R explains where to find the target.
- **Reward names:** the marker accounts for story progress and collected treasures. Rewards rolled when opening the chest are labelled as chances; a spot with no remaining treasure says so.
- **World-map autowalk:** steering accounts for the character's collision size, integer movement and camera direction. Local recovery repairs reproduced stalls on the approach to Old Man's House and Buggy steering loops. When approaching the house in the Buggy, navigation asks the player to continue on foot and keeps the route selected.
- **Control pauses and field exits:** native menus and focus changes release automatic movement and clear stale steering history. Field doorway approaches allow room for the character's collision size, repairing the reproduced Under Junon exit loop.

## Testing

The release is checked with installed game data in both runtime suites, shared and parity suites, package and native dependency validation, and Ghidra evidence tied to the portable archive. Dig tests cover all eight possible destinations from both levels, ladder action prompts, correct ground-triangle arrival, collected-item flags and reward gates. An independent check compares 640 reward states per runtime with the native payout script. World-map replay tests cover 432 house approaches and 216 Buggy escapes per runtime.

These automated and native-data checks are not live playthroughs. Please test a complete excavation, the approach to Old Man's House, and resuming autowalk after opening and closing a menu. Other unverified navigation problems may remain.
