# Blind Soldier 0.6.9

This prerelease improves Story navigation, NPC discovery, exits and auto walk throughout the game for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- Repairs the shared game-script reader so navigation can discover previously skipped exits and movement paths, follow native conditions, and distinguish the player's movement from other characters' animations.
- Adds 70 scripted exit definitions and 1,129 traversal definitions, and removes 183 invalid traversal definitions. These are catalog changes, not a claim that every route has been played through.
- Applies supported live game-state conditions to 115 scripted exits and respects native gateway switches.
- Adds contact-based NPC discovery and keeps auto walk approaching until the game registers the interaction. It remembers contact during dialogue so movement stays stopped afterward.
- Corrects Bugenhagen's contact step in Cosmo Canyon, the Gold Saucer information counter, Lucrecia's reward conditions, exits at Fort Condor's summit and Junon's Respectable Inn, the returning Junon dolphin step, and the later Corel pursuit's upper and lower track routes.

The audit covered all 702 mapped fields and the world and battle script resources. Both full runtime suites, the shared-layout and parity suites, and all 280 Story checkpoints passed. The packaged runtime DLLs also passed independent script, exit-condition and contact-interaction checks.

Live gameplay testing is still needed. This release does not certify that every save state, optional route or minigame is completable. Some optional-object approaches in Sector 7, Cosmo Canyon and Ancient Forest remain unverified, and the Great Glacier map view is still undescribed. The [audit report](https://github.com/buu420/blind-soldier/blob/v0.6.9/analysis/2026-09-24-whole-game-engine-audit.md) records the scope and remaining limits.
