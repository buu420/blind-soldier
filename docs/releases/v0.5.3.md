# Blind Soldier 0.5.3

This prerelease expands story guidance, descriptions and minigame feedback, with
repairs for navigation problems found during playtesting through the first Gold
Saucer visit and Corel Prison chocobo race.

## Changes

- **Story guidance:** 1110 definitions now cover required story routes from the
  opening through the ending. Later chapters have offline checks and still need
  playtesting; this does not mean the entire game is fully accessible or validated.
- **Junon and the journey onward:** improved parade and drill cues, post-practice
  story exits, cargo ship chest and exit navigation, and routes and objects through
  Costa del Sol, Mount Corel and North Corel.
- **Gold Saucer:** expanded story targets and descriptions, corrected Speed Square
  registration guidance, and native feedback for the coaster, G-Bike, 3D Battler,
  arm wrestling, basketball and Chocobo Square activities.
- **Movie descriptions:** narration for the first cable-car arrival at Gold Saucer
  and five gondola movies, in addition to the opening movie. Descriptions generated
  with ViddyScribe were reviewed against the game's visuals; attribution is included.
- **Chocobo races:** automatic/manual mode, accepted acceleration and slowing,
  dashing, stamina and your place are now spoken. Pace announcements are spaced to
  avoid flooding. Press **K** to reread race status. Rival-only position swaps no
  longer repeatedly interrupt a player race with the full order.
- **Autowalk:** interaction approaches now account for the player's collision
  radius, unreachable static exits disabled by native scripts are filtered, and a
  walk that makes no progress for five seconds stops with a spoken explanation.
  Dialogue, ladders and scripted pauses are handled separately.
- Additional Fort Condor readout improvements, world-map entrance and terrain
  feedback, timing cues and shared support across both runtime architectures.

## Coverage and known limits

Live playtesting has reached the first Gold Saucer visit and Corel Prison race.
Later story guidance has offline verification, but later chapters and optional
content need further testing and accessibility work. Steam 2026 x64 needs broader
live testing. Most FMVs still have no recorded audio description.

The latest autowalk repairs were verified with offline route and movement tests.
Holding Run and brief back-and-forth movement at some corners still need live
testing; please report remaining problem locations.

Four runtime test suites passed. Both portable archives passed their manifest,
architecture, dependency and extraction checks; Ghidra verified their native
bootstrap and proxy components. The Chocobo/autowalk build also passed 80 focused
package checks. Nine older installer integration cases could not run because their
required Steam 2026 game fixture was unavailable; this release makes no new claim
of live Steam 2026 compatibility testing.

## Downloads and updating

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** both runtime architectures, including Steam 2026.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** the legacy 2013/7th Heaven package.
- **ffviinew-v0.5.3-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.5.3-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod
  Manager packages. Use these through the manager.

SHA-256 files accompany every archive. For portable installation, extract into
the appropriate game folder following the included README, then launch normally
through Steam or 7th Heaven. When updating, back up and restore
`Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration\config.json` to keep
your settings; the ZIP includes a default configuration.
