# Blind Soldier 0.4.0 (Beta)

This beta makes field navigation trustworthy through the Midgar slums, the
Mythril Mine and Fort Condor, gives the PHS screen its voice back, and warns
players when they step onto the Midgar Zolom's marsh.

## What changed

### Navigation

- Stops announcing one field boundary as several identical exits. A boundary is
  often stored as a chain of gateway records, and each segment was announced
  separately, so the same doorway was heard two, three, sometimes six times with
  no way to tell the copies apart. Duplicate spoken exit labels across fields
  1-470 drop from 98 to 64, and every field from the Midgar slums through Junon
  is now unambiguous.
- Completes an exit route where the game actually fires it. A native gateway
  only triggers when the party crosses its line, but arrival used the ordinary
  radius, so navigation announced "reached" and released auto-walk while the
  player was still short of it - up to 122 units short in the Honey Bee Inn
  lobby, where the room then never opened.
- Follows the two ways FF7 encodes a vine climb, so the Mythril Mine's upper
  ledge joins the walkmesh graph in both directions. The Junon-side mine mouth
  and the Long Range Materia above it were reported unreachable, and the exit
  was hidden, so there was no way to be guided out of the mine at all.
- Names every Mythril Mine gateway rather than labelling all four screens "Exit
  to Mythril Mine" and both world-map mouths a bare "Exit".
- Anchors scripted triggers to their own storey. In a field built as a tower,
  a trigger whose height belongs to an upper floor was being attached to the
  floor underneath it, which in Fort Condor's save room produced a ladder that
  appeared to lead up out of the floor: auto-walk delivered the player to the
  point beneath it and then paced back and forth with no move able to make
  progress. Two of that field's fifteen transitions were affected.
- Holds the route and the exit list while a ladder or a script is moving the
  player, instead of recomputing them. A climb travels vertically while the
  planner reasons horizontally, so mid-climb the route was rebuilt with its next
  waypoint back at the mount point and auto-walk turned the player around.

### Fort Condor

- Names the villagers, shop staff, elder and lookout. The whole region announced
  no NPCs at all.
- Names every route in and out of the fort, the entrance, the watch room and the
  mountain top.
- Keeps a mounted climb pointed the way the game says it goes, instead of
  deriving a direction from a route waypoint behind the player and calling an
  upward ladder "climb left".

### Menus

- Restores speech on the PHS screen. It draws no module of its own and runs
  inside whatever module raised it, so the screen was never recognised as open
  and arrowing around it said nothing at all. Reserve members are named from the
  native roster, since the game draws them as portraits rather than text.
- Reads Sense results only once they are complete, and fails closed on partial
  or unresolved stats so an unsensed enemy's details stay private.
- Isolates main-menu speech ownership so unrelated overlays cannot claim it.

### World map

- Announces stepping onto and leaving the Midgar Zolom's marsh, and names the
  Zolom while it is still on the map. The marsh is its own terrain and the Zolom
  is visible crossing it, so without this the first notice a player got was the
  battle transition.

### Under the hood

- The Steam 2026 x64 release gate now runs the title and load-menu suite, which
  it previously skipped - a real failure there could have reached a release
  unnoticed.
- Adds a diagnostic that locates the Fort Condor battle minigame's live state.
  That battle draws none of its interface through any routine the mod can hook,
  so its menus remain silent for now and need a purpose-built reader.

## Testing requested

Both runtimes need eyes, and **Steam 2026 x64 especially** - these changes are
verified on legacy x86 and by test suite on x64, but not yet in a real x64
playthrough.

- Walk the Midgar slums, Wall Market and the Honey Bee Inn lobby and confirm
  each exit is announced once, with a name that distinguishes it from its
  neighbours, and that auto-walk carries you through the doorway rather than
  stopping short of it.
- Cross the Mythril Mine in both directions, including the vine to the upper
  ledge and the Materia above it.
- In Fort Condor, climb out of the save room with auto-walk, visit the shops and
  the elder, and go up to the watch room.
- Walk onto the Midgar Zolom's marsh on foot and confirm it is announced.
- Open PHS from the field, the world map and the main menu, and arrow through
  both the active party and the reserve list.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching prerelease-channel update directly.
