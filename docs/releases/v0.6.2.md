# Blind Soldier 0.6.2

This prerelease expands and repairs Story, town and exploration navigation for both legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- Repairs Nibelheim mansion progression and restores mansion door routes used during the flashback.
- Adds missing routes for the return to Gold Saucer and later Highwind visits, and corrects Temple and Lifestream targets and availability.
- Adds Great Glacier guidance through 26 fields, using the game's current corridor state to continue toward the northern snowfield.
- Adds 65 missing objects/interactions and 15 NPC labels across towns and optional areas, including Wutai and the Ancient Forest. Native visibility and interaction state still control which entries appear.
- Makes all seven Gold Saucer Terminal Floor platforms available in Exits, independently of the current Story objective.
- Fixes auto walk alternating between arrived and resumed at some interaction approaches, and the North Corel doorway search timing out before returning a usable route.

Both full runtime test suites passed. Native checks covered 702 fields, 54 selected progression states across both runtimes and both installed archives, plus the new interaction and route cases. This is not a completed live playthrough. Optional puzzles, including the Great Glacier ice-floe jumps and Ancient Forest carrying/placement, still require the game's controls.

Existing movie narration and all 846 short narration clips are preserved. No new descriptions are added in this release.

- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy 2013/7th Heaven x86.
