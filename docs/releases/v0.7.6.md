# Blind Soldier 0.7.6

This testing release covers Icicle Inn, the story snowboard run, and Great Glacier, with menu and battle speech repairs for both legacy x86 and Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

## Changes

- **Icicle Inn:** added the video player to Objects so Gast's optional recordings can be reached. Checked the existing snowboard, map, and departure sequence against the game's scripts.
- **Story snowboarding:** brief cues identify bends, forks, the selected route, pauses, and the finish. Cues follow the currently visible course. This does not add automatic steering.
- **Great Glacier:** added the snowfield's four edges and central cave to Locations, clearer destination names for exits, and Objects for the hot spring and both ice-floe starting points. Checked the routes and native availability conditions for all seven treasures, Snow, Alexander, and Holzoff's cabin.
- **Glacier map and ice floes:** the map screen has a spoken description. The ice-floe crossing reads the current position, facing, and neighboring landing options. Press **R** during the crossing to hear the full visible grid. Use the game's normal controls to jump.
- **Key Items and Limit:** Key Items now reads the selected name and description, including both columns and scrolling. Limit announces the selected character's current level and gauge, and identifies unavailable levels.
- **Steam x64 battle speech:** full capture queues can recover instead of permanently stopping battle speech. Brief mapping interruptions are tolerated, and repeated blinking LIMIT BREAK messages no longer keep interrupting themselves. Genuine capture failures retain diagnostics.
- **Old Man's House:** improved entrance approaches, proximity cues, and navigation around parked vehicles. Leaving space between the vehicle and the doorway still matters.

## Testing

Full legacy, Steam x64, shared, and parity suites passed against the packaged runtime DLLs. Native-data checks cover 601 snowboard positions, three forks and four endings; 96 Glacier exit chains and 88 walkable arrivals; 18 optional approaches; 25 snowfield routes; all seven treasure scripts; and 5,120 ice-floe direction cases. Both native arrivals can reach the Icicle Inn video player.

These are automated and native-data checks, not a live playthrough. Please test Icicle Inn's progression and recordings, the snowboard cues, Glacier exploration and optional items, battles, and the Limit and Key Items menus. The historical cause of every reported speech failure is not proven.

## Downloads and updating

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** Steam x64 portable package, also containing the dual-runtime mod.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy/7th Heaven portable package.
- **ffviinew-v0.7.6-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.7.6-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

For a manual ZIP update, back up the entire `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration` folder, extract the matching ZIP over the game folder, then restore that folder. This preserves your settings and room-description history. Recorded narration and saves are unchanged.
