# Blind Soldier 0.10.1

Priscilla now has a selectable NPC target at Dolphin Cove when playing with
Echo-S 7. Navigation uses her current position and the game's interaction range.
Her existing beach Story guidance remains available.

During the Kalm flashback, wounded Tifa now appears in NPCs and Story inside the
reactor. Navigation approaches the native interaction target for her injured pose.
Use your normal Confirm button to continue the scene. Once it advances, Story
guides you toward Jenova's chamber.

Both FFVII runtimes are covered. Native archive checks exercise stock data and
the five reviewed Echo-S variants; Steam checks use its checked navigation
planner. See [validation](../validation/character-interactions-20261015.md).
Live gameplay, physical controller input and audible Prism output have not been
confirmed for these fixes.

Close the game before updating. Saves, settings, narration history and existing
audio-description recordings are preserved. This release adds no recordings.
