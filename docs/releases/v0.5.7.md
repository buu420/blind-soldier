# Blind Soldier 0.5.7

This prerelease restores missing North Corel NPC navigation and addresses session-long party-menu silence reported in Steam 2026 x64.

## Changes

- North Corel's main town and three homes and inn now identify 19 native residents, shopkeepers and animals without requiring a speaker name in their dialogue. Shop and seated-villager targets use the game's actual interaction lines. Hidden models and disabled interactions remain excluded.
- A full x64 menu observation buffer now discards the incomplete batch, resets menu speech state, and resumes on fresh observations. It no longer permanently disables party-menu speech for the rest of the session.
- A failed x64 menu health check immediately pauses observation and retries. Capture resumes only when the native identities validate again; three consecutive failures still retire the hooks. Observations spanning a failure and recovery are rejected.
- Logs distinguish sustained menu validation failure from callback exceptions, and record buffer recovery.

## Validation and limits

Tests cover resident visibility and interaction gates, installed North Corel scripts and counter geometry, menu buffer recovery, transient and sustained validation loss, and capture races. Ghidra checks confirmed the native visibility, Talk, LINE and menu handlers. Both architecture builds and release checks were run.

The friend's log proves that party-menu capture shut down before later Fort Condor visits. Its old diagnostic does not distinguish a full buffer, a failed health check, or a callback exception. The transient buffer and health-check cases are now reproduced in tests and corrected; the friend still needs to retest the actual session after updating and restarting.

North Corel coverage in this patch is the ordinary town and interiors, not its later story-event variants. Narration assets and description coverage are unchanged from 0.5.6. Live story playtesting has reached the first Gold Saucer visit and Corel Prison race; later guidance has not had a complete playthrough.

## Downloads and updating

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** both runtime architectures, including Steam 2026 x64.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy 2013/7th Heaven x86 package.
- **ffviinew-v0.5.7-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.5.7-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

Each archive includes a SHA-256 sidecar. Preserve `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration\config.json` when updating a portable installation to keep your settings. Restart the game to load the update.
