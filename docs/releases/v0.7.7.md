# Blind Soldier 0.7.7

This testing release repairs Shinra Mansion's coffin-room exits and the reported autowalk stalls, and adds missing optional interactions found during a guide review. It supports legacy x86 and Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

## Changes

- **Vincent's room:** Exits now includes "Enter the coffin room" and "Leave the coffin room" in the basement. These follow the native key and door state and the side of the doorway you are standing on. The selected route remains active while crossing the threshold. Vincent remains an optional encounter.
- **Autowalk:** routes account for the player's body when passing narrow gaps and rounding walls. This repairs the logged Nibelheim and mansion stalls, including running. NPC approaches can finish within native talking range and turn toward the NPC before stopping, reducing oscillation at counters. Press the game's normal OK button to interact.
- **Rocket Town:** navigation recognizes the door in Cid's house that releases two connected sections of the walkway together.
- **Optional interactions:** added 26 Objects entries and repaired Tifa's piano, including Wonder Square machines, Battle Square points windows, Honey Bee Inn doors, the rocket panel, Mideel examination spots, and several other native interaction points. Four NPC counter targets now use their actual interaction lines. Forgotten City's rest prompts give clearer instructions.

## Testing and coverage

Reviewed all 43 chronological walkthrough chapters, including optional content, against the catalogs and native field scripts. The [guide audit](https://github.com/buu420/blind-soldier/blob/v0.7.7/tools/GuideRouteAudit/guide-state-audit-2026-09-28.md) records the checks and remaining uncertainties.

Full legacy, Steam x64, shared, and parity suites passed against the tested runtime DLLs. Targeted native movement replays cover the reported routes, coffin-room crossing, innkeeper counter, and Cid's house door. Static route comparisons cover 702 map entries against both installed field archives, with no regression in matched route classifications.

A complete live playthrough remains unverified. Static route checks do not establish every live script condition, actor collision, or slope traversal. Please retest the mansion's basement Exits and autowalk with walking and running.

For a manual ZIP update, back up the entire `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration` folder, extract the matching ZIP over the game folder, then restore that folder. This preserves settings and room-description history. Recorded narration and saves are unchanged.
