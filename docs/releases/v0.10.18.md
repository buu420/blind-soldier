# Blind Soldier 0.10.18 beta

- After feeding pitcher plant 2 in Ancient Forest, hold **Left** to jump back to the bank. On a controller, release the navigation trigger first. Story resumes from the landing, and Auto Walk continues toward the next insect if it was enabled.
- **Objects** offers **Jump back to the bank** from plant 1. Select a visible insect on the bank and the same selection will carry through the jump. Cycling the single **Story** step now keeps Auto Walk enabled.
- For live testing, feed plant 2, re-read the Story step, make the Left jump, and check that Auto Walk takes you to the remaining insect. The complete timed crossing and the reported Run input still need live testing.

This beta is available for Steam 2026 x64 and FFVII 2013 x86, including 7th Heaven. Close the game before updating. If you use 7th Heaven, close it as well.
