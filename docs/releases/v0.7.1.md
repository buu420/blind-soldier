# Blind Soldier 0.7.1

This prerelease improves field navigation and interaction approaches for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64, following a chapter-by-chapter audit of the walkthrough against the game's native field data.

- **Added Effect in the Cave of the Gi:** navigation follows the route through the neighboring cave and back to the upper ledge. The selected destination survives field transitions and battle interruptions; explicitly stopping autowalk cancels continuation.
- **Don Corneo's bedroom:** when Cloud is selected, Story leads to the bedside OK interaction used by the game, with the correct event state and stopping distance.
- **Counters and background objects:** autowalk checks the native interaction line and the player's collision radius before announcing arrival. It no longer stops at a generic distance outside the usable range. This also applies to the relevant Story interactions.
- **Other corrected approaches:** the observatory and cargo-room fallback endpoints, Train Graveyard barrels, Sector 5 television and bedroom conversations, and Gongaga's Turks encounter.
- **Materia caves:** the Mime, Quadra Magic and Knights of Round crystals now appear in Objects when their native interactions are available and the items remain uncollected.
- **Ancient Forest and Mega All:** targets use verified take-off or throwing positions. Conditional plant jumps and damaging flytraps are no longer assumed to be ordinary paths. Timed button presses and puzzle actions remain manual.

The audit maps all 43 walkthrough chapters and 293 checkpoints to the game fields. It evaluates 702 readable fields in both runtime assemblies against both installed archives, preserving separate entrance and target-state evidence. Both complete runtime suites and the native Story/transit checks passed against the packaged DLLs. The [audit report](https://github.com/buu420/blind-soldier/blob/v0.7.1/docs/guide-route-audit-20260925.md) and [chapter ledger](https://github.com/buu420/blind-soldier/blob/v0.7.1/docs/guide-route-audit-ledger-20260925.md) record the corrections and unresolved states.

Live testing is still needed. This audit does not certify a complete playthrough or every moving actor, party state, puzzle or world-map route. Some state-dependent approaches remain unverified rather than being treated as working.
