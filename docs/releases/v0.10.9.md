# Blind Soldier 0.10.9 beta

Adds Ancient Forest puzzle feedback and repairs unsafe flytrap routing on both runtimes.

- **Objects:** insects, frogs and pitcher plants have distinct local numbers. The raised throwing spots on the first screen name the plant they face, including the return throw. Available jumps retain their native direction prompts; ordinary vine and stamen routes remain usable.
- **Puzzle feedback:** hear what you carry, plants closing, visibly shaking and opening, flytrap closures and visible creature changes. Repeat gives the current state. Unheard current facts survive interruptions, and routine directions give them time to finish. Puzzle choices, releasing creatures and button-triggered jumps use normal game controls.
- **Flytraps:** routes avoid active jaws instead of repeatedly running into them. Supershot ST has guidance to its normal interaction range outside the jaws. Movement near a trap has a separate cutoff during slow scans. When another automatic step cannot be checked safely, it announces a manual handoff and keeps spoken guidance active. Destinations behind the puzzle stay unavailable on foot until you reach their side.
- **Echo-S:** restores the two missing frogs on the first screen, excludes bite animations from crossing routes and describes the changed Down-button action correctly. All five reviewed layouts use the same checks.

The forest contains Apocalypse, along with its other native rewards. Collecting items and restarting at the forest entrance retain the game's normal behavior.

Close the game before updating. Saves, settings, description histories, recordings and unrelated mods are preserved. Native-data and automated checks cover both runtimes and five Echo-S layouts. These checks are separate from live traversal, physical-controller and audible screen-reader testing.
