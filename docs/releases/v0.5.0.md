# Blind Soldier 0.5.0 (Beta)

Two things: the accessible launcher no longer crashes, and Fort Condor can be
played by ear.

If you are on 0.4.1 and the launcher closed the moment you opened it, that is
fixed here and it was not your machine.

## What changed

### The accessible launcher

- Starts instead of closing immediately. 0.4.1 updated the speech library the
  launcher loads but not the declaration describing how to talk to it, and the
  mismatch corrupted the stack before the launcher had chosen a screen reader at
  all. It affected every 0.4.1 user of the launcher on both the 2013 and 2026
  packages, with any screen reader or none, which is why nobody could have
  worked around it. The game itself was never affected - starting from Steam or
  7th Heaven always worked.

### Fort Condor

The fort battle draws every word of its interface as a picture, so there has
never been any text to intercept. All of the following is rebuilt from the
battle's own memory.

- Reads the hire list: each unit's name, price, HP, attack, speed and what it is
  good for, and says when you cannot afford one.
- Says where a unit can actually be placed, and how far. Not just "clear" for the
  spot you are on, but the extent of the band you are in - "placeable 20 up and
  36 down, 1 more band" - because the ground has real gaps and sweeping for them
  by ear is not how a sighted player finds them.
- Reports the battle while it happens. Units going down on either side, with what
  is left standing, and the enemy advance gauge at quarters. That gauge is drawn
  on screen for the whole battle and is the one thing a sighted player glances at
  to know they are losing; it was going entirely unsaid.
- Says who won. The banner reads "Enemy invasion." whether or not you know what
  that means, so the result is now said in plain words as well.
- Names the units on the field, including the enemy's - Commander, Wyvern, Beast
  and Barbarian - taken from the labels the game itself draws.
- All of it works on both the 2013 and 2026 runtimes. The fort was read only on
  the older one when this work started.

### Under the hood

- The placement region is checked against the battlefield's real collision mesh,
  read out of the game's own archive, so a future change that breaks it fails the
  build rather than reaching a player.
- A build guard now fails if a reader or speech tracker is compiled into one
  runtime and not the other. This is a dual-runtime mod and the fort reader
  reached players on one runtime only; that is the class of mistake the guard
  exists to stop.

## Known limits

- The enemy advance gauge is reported at quarters, not continuously.
- Ten of the unit-name slots in the game's own artwork read "dummy". They are
  reported as such, because that is the label a sighted player would see.
