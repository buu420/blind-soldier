# Blind Soldier 0.7.8

This testing release repairs navigation through the icicle caves at Gaea's Cliff and includes the recent battle narration and menu speech improvements. It supports legacy x86 and Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

## Changes

- **Gaea's Cliff progression:** after the three required icicles, Story points toward the fallen-ice crossing that continues the route. Twenty-two cave exits now distinguish passages and levels. A used-up first-icicle jump prompt disappears from the exit list according to the game's current state.
- **Climbing and autowalk:** long climbs track progress toward the chosen landing instead of stopping after five seconds while the character is still moving. Sloped-field routes allow more clearance from angled walls, including the tight corner on the Ribbon approach. The Enhance Sword entry explains its route through the adjoining room.
- **Limit Break and summon descriptions:** 93 distinct animations have 175 recorded visual cues in Brice's approved voice, with longer attacks described in stages. An additional cue describes the shared summoning effect, and the displayed summon attack name is read on each cast. Each animation and the shared opening are described once per save. Narration follows the animation and pauses with battle pause. Set `EnableBattleAnimationDescriptions` to `false` to disable these recordings.
- **Battle feedback:** HP and MP drains are identified as drains, and visible damage and status results are announced when they are displayed instead of at action selection.
- **Menus and dialogue:** Materia Arrange and Check selections are read. Native dialogue choices have more reliable text and page handling, including the reported Nibelheim choices.

## Testing

The release passed the automated dual-runtime gate, native loader and Ghidra checks, and full legacy, Steam x64, shared, and parity suites against the packaged runtime DLLs. Cave-specific checks cover story conditions, climb progress, jump availability, and body clearance. A bounded Ribbon movement simulation reached the chest's interaction range at walking and running speeds.

These checks do not replace a live playthrough. Please retest the cliff climbs, fallen-ice jumps, Ribbon approach, and summon opening and narration timing in both game versions.

## Downloads and updating

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** Steam x64 portable package, also containing the dual-runtime mod.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy/7th Heaven portable package.
- **ffviinew-v0.7.8-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.7.8-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

For a manual ZIP update, back up the entire `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration` folder, extract the matching ZIP over the game folder, then restore that folder. This preserves settings and description history. Existing movie and cutscene recordings are retained, and saves are unchanged.
