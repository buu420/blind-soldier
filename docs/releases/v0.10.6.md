# Blind Soldier 0.10.6 beta

Repairs ladder navigation during the return to Midgar on Disc 2.

- Available scaffold ladders are selectable in Exits. Routes guide you to the native mount and through the climb; use your normal action and direction buttons.
- A ladder that starts as you enter a screen remains understandable even with no walking route active. Steam now gives the same native climb and return-direction speech as the 2013 runtime.
- The subway entrance ladder offers both manual ends, including after the Turks encounter.
- Scaffold ducts, ladders, walkways and tunnel junctions have distinct labels. The unlocked ladder toward Shinra HQ includes the climb before the field transfer.
- Browsing an item while on a ladder identifies your current climb separately from the selected target.
- Return visits retain the existing Story guidance while those routes remain available, with the native encounter, bridge and tunnel-section restrictions.

The Aegis Armlet remains subject to the game's one-way collapsing walkway. Navigation guides the available native approaches; it cannot return to the armlet's platform after that route is closed.

Both runtimes advance to 0.10.6, with stock and Echo-S compatibility checks. Saves, player settings, description histories and existing recordings are preserved. Live traversal, physical controller use and audible Prism feedback have not been tested in this development session.
