# Blind Soldier 0.8.0

This testing release improves Gold Saucer exploration and G-Bike steering, and adds Highwind navigation for legacy x86 and Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

## Gold Saucer

- Restores the four first-floor Wonder Square machines to the Objects list on return visits, with interaction targets that follow the game's current state.
- Improves approaches around attendants, route corners, and hotel exits, and repairs the automatic-movement state used by field navigation.
- G-Bike auto steering prioritizes enemies threatening the truck, keeps more room from the road edges, and holds attack alignment during a manual swing. Attacks remain under your control; game scoring is unchanged.

## Highwind

- Selecting a reachable location or Story destination while flying plans a route to nearby grass that supports landing and a walk to the destination. Routes account for the airship's landing footprint, dismount position, and parked vehicles.
- On arrival, auto walk brakes, waits for the airship to settle, and announces the landing spot. **Press Cancel to land manually.** The mod does not press the landing button.
- After landing and dismounting, the destination remains selected. If auto walk is enabled, it continues on foot. Cancelling navigation ends the route.
- Destinations without a valid grass landing and walking route report that limitation. Routes do not unlock areas that require another vehicle or story progress.

The regional Great Glacier treasure navigation from 0.7.9 remains included, along with the existing recordings and narration history support.

## Testing and remaining limits

Checks cover both runtime suites, installed game data, portable packages, native loading, and Ghidra verification. Additional checks exercise Gold Saucer machine approaches and native movement legs, plus Highwind flight across camera orientations and update intervals, landing headings, braking, and airborne arrival handling.

These are automated checks, not a complete live playthrough. Please test Wonder Square machines and hotel routes, G-Bike steering, and Highwind arrival, manual landing, and dismount in both game versions. Improved steering does not guarantee a winning G-Bike score.

## Downloads and updating

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** Steam x64 portable package, also containing the dual-runtime mod.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy/7th Heaven portable package.
- **ffviinew-v0.8.0-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.8.0-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

For a manual ZIP update, back up the entire `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration` folder, extract the matching ZIP over the game folder, then restore that folder. This preserves settings and description history. Existing recordings and saves are unchanged.
