# Blind Soldier 0.10.12 beta

Open **Story** in the Ancient Forest to follow one puzzle step at a time. The
guide tells you which insects to stage, where to throw each creature, when to
hold a direction to jump, and when to wait for a frog to launch you. It speaks
the next step as the puzzle changes while Story is selected. Use your usual
navigation controls to follow a target, then the normal game buttons to act.

The route covers all seven treasures: Supershot ST, Spring Gun Clip, Slash-All,
Typoon, Minerva Band, Apocalypse, and Elixir. If you use the game's Switch
reset, Story picks up from the entrance and skips rewards you already collected.
Objects remains available for exploring the puzzle on your own.

Close the game before updating. This beta covers both the 2013 and Steam 2026
versions of Final Fantasy VII.
