# Blind Soldier 1.0.4

This update increases audio buffering to help devices that experience skipping
or stuttering during Blind Soldier's sounds and recorded descriptions.

- Descriptions, navigation sounds and footsteps now queue 160 ms of audio
  across four buffers, up from 80 ms across two.
- Timed cues use a smaller 80 ms queue across four buffers, up from 40 ms across
  two. Each refill stays at 20 ms for timed cues and 40 ms for other sounds.

The larger queues tolerated short simulated audio-source stalls in local tests.
Both runtime regression suites passed. The reported external sound card was
not available for testing, so please report whether this improves playback on
your device. Larger queues can add a small delay to newly requested cues.
