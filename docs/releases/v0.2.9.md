# Blind Soldier 0.2.9 (Beta)

This beta restores the remaining Nibelheim flashback progression and choice
speech, and brings the Steam 2026 x64 Order menu up to the same native-backed
behavior as the 2013 x86 runtime.

## What changed

- Adds the missing Story objectives after the first Nibel Reactor sequence,
  through the Shinra Mansion halls, spiral stairs, basement corridor, library,
  and the return to Sephiroth.
- Recognizes the verified original-game field-script variants used throughout
  this section, so the new Story objectives remain tied to actual game state.
- Captures choice menus that call the original ASK handler behind FFNx's voice
  wrapper, including Cloud's conversation with his mother in the flashback.
- Preserves the FFNx wrapper lifecycle while preventing duplicate choice
  announcements when the wrapper forwards to the original handler.
- Makes the Steam 2026 x64 Order menu announce the selected party member's
  front or back row, pending swap selection, and the member chosen to swap.
- Uses the same native Order-menu state reader on both supported runtimes.

## Testing requested

Continue the flashback after the first Nibel Reactor visit. Confirm Story leads
through the mansion to the library and that Cloud's dialogue choices with his
mother announce normally without repeating.

On Steam 2026 x64, open the main menu and choose Order. Confirm each party
member and front/back row speaks, selecting a member announces the pending
selection, and choosing another member announces the swap correctly.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching beta-channel update directly.
