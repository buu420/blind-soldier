# Blind Soldier 0.2.6 (Beta)

This beta patch repairs the Floor 63 coupon puzzle and its crawlspace
navigation in both the 2013 x86 and Steam 2026 x64 runtimes.

## What changed

- Tracks the A, B, and C Coupon sequence with the game's persistent coupon
  state instead of temporary duct-entry bits that the game clears on load.
- Keeps the B Coupon and final coupon-exchange objectives available after
  returning from the crawlspace.
- Clears stale target selection when moving between the Floor 63 rooms and
  crawlspace so Story selects the nearest current objective.
- Follows the live route through crawlspace bends even when the game reports
  the crawl as ladder-style movement, preventing repeated directions at an
  elbow.
- Applies the same corrected Story and Object state to both supported game
  runtimes.

## Testing requested

On Floor 63, collect the A Coupon, enter its duct, follow Story to the B Coupon
shaft, collect B and C, then use the second duct to return to the computer.
Confirm Story and Objects advance at each room transition and that navigation
turns through the crawlspace instead of retaining the previous direction.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching beta-channel update directly.
