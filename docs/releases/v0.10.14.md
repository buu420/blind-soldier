# Blind Soldier 0.10.14 beta

- **Ancient Forest:** Fed pitcher plants stay closed roughly twice as long, including with FFNx at 60 FPS. The shaking cue still signals that a plant is about to reopen or launch you. Use **Story** to follow the crossing one step at a time.
- **Parked chocobo:** After dismounting an owned chocobo on the world map, choose **Transportation → Parked chocobo** to get back into boarding range. Press **Confirm** when prompted.

Close the game before updating. This beta is available for Steam 2026 and the 2013 version used with 7th Heaven. Please let us know whether the extra time is enough to finish the crossing and whether you can find and remount your chocobo after leaving a Materia Cave.
