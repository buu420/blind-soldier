# Blind Soldier 0.6.6

This prerelease fixes Tiny Bronco Transportation navigation for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64.

- Keeps parked vehicles listed after disembarking, even when no walking route is currently available. Selecting an unreachable vehicle reports that the route is unavailable.
- Finds a boarding approach on the reachable shore instead of choosing the opposite bank or stopping at the center of a large triangle.
- Stops auto walk when the game records contact with the selected vehicle. Press the game's normal Confirm button to board.
- Updates the approach when a parked vehicle moves and stops steering when the selected vehicle disappears or is boarded.
- Prevents ordinary movement of world-map entities from discarding the entire Transportation list.

Both full runtime regression suites and all 12 simulated speed/camera combinations passed. These checks use native map data and the game's collision behavior; actual in-game boarding with this build still needs testing.
