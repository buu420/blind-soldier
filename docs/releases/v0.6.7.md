# Blind Soldier 0.6.7

This prerelease fixes Wutai navigation and the Manipulate action menu for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64. It also includes the weapon-seller and Tiny Bronco improvements since 0.6.6.

- Corrects the route through Wutai's mountain pass and across its bridges. Walking routes avoid bridge sides and leave room around terrain corners; steering follows the route more closely. Wutai's entrance now prompts riders to dismount instead of announcing arrival while the game refuses entry.
- Reads the controlled enemy's action choices after Manipulate succeeds, including cursor changes and subsequent turns. Names come from the installed battle scene; hidden enemy statistics remain hidden.
- Restores the weapon seller's conversation and exit under Story at his house near Gongaga. The NPC target follows the seller instead of pointing to a reward box. The reward boxes and bed appear as separate Objects when the game permits their use.
- Keeps Tiny Bronco routes on navigable water, improves steering through bends, and guides shore approaches using the game's landing rules. At a confirmed landing position, press the game's normal Cancel button to disembark.
- Corrects navigation into the weapon seller's world-map entrance without routing through unrelated entrances.

Both full runtime suites passed against the packaged mod DLLs. Automated checks also cover the seller's 325 floor routes, Tiny Bronco landing trips, and 40 Wutai start/camera replays. A wider 260-walk comparison found no new failures among routes the previous build could complete. These checks use installed game data and modeled native movement rules; they do not replace live gameplay testing.
