# Blind Soldier 0.2.8 (Beta)

This beta expands the Nibelheim flashback guidance, restores battle-menu
speech for scripted guest parties, and stops the party-formation prompt from
interrupting member and slot announcements.

## What changed

- Adds ordered Story objectives through the Nibelheim flashback, including the
  inn, Mt. Nibel, both reactor visits, the Shinra Mansion basement, and the
  return route during the final confrontation.
- Keeps the two-level Nibel Reactor entrance routes on their correct walkmesh
  components so Story directs Cloud to the ladder before the lower doorway.
- Adds Tifa's piano as a trackable Object during the flashback.
- Reads battle commands and party information when the game uses scripted
  guest character IDs, including Sephiroth in the Nibelheim flashback.
- Treats the normal Reform selection instruction as passive text, so temporary
  validation messages no longer alternate continuously or starve party member
  and slot speech.
- Applies the battle and menu fixes to both the 2013 x86 and Steam 2026 x64
  runtimes.

## Testing requested

Follow Story from Nibelheim into the inn, across Mt. Nibel, through the reactor
and mansion, and back to the reactor. Confirm the route switches cleanly at the
reactor ladder and that Tifa's piano appears under Objects.

During the scripted flashback battle, confirm the active party member and battle
commands speak normally. On a Reform or choose-party screen, trigger the
three-member validation message, then move through the party slots and reserve
members; each name and slot should speak without the validation prompt
repeating over it.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching beta-channel update directly.
