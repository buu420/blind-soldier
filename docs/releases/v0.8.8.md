# Blind Soldier 0.8.8

This beta adds automatic world-map diving and repairs submarine destinations on
legacy x86/7th Heaven and native Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

- While sailing on the surface, choose an underwater destination in Locations
  and start auto walk. Navigation sails to suitable Sea terrain, uses the game's
  configured Cancel action to dive, and continues toward your chosen destination
  after the underwater map loads. Events also offers Dive underwater.
- Deep sea is not a valid dive entry in the native game. Spoken guidance now
  names Cancel instead of Confirm/X. Ordinary guidance keeps diving manual;
  automatic travel performs the dive for you.
- Locations includes the Sunken Gelnika, the present Key of the Ancients, the
  present red submarine wreck and Lucrecia's Cave lake. Story names the Huge
  Materia wreck while its Materia remains uncollected. Existing surface events
  and story destinations remain available.
- The route through the underwater tunnel reaches a safe surfacing point in
  Lucrecia's lake. Press Cancel to surface, select Submarine pen in Regions, then
  face the shore and press Cancel to get off. Enter the cave on foot. Small
  submarine pens are now included in Regions.
- Navigation to the Gelnika, Key and wreck still stops above their entrance and
  asks you to descend with normal controls. Surfacing and boarding remain manual.
- Walking to Highwind now reaches the native boarding contact before announcing
  arrival. Press Confirm to board. Selecting Highwind from the submarine explains
  that you must dock and get off first.

Use the usual R3 navigation menu and A/X for automatic travel. Keyboard controls
remain J/L to choose, I for spoken guidance, P for auto walk and K to repeat.
Automatic diving reads your current game bindings; it never assumes a physical
button and stops with an explanation if the bindings cannot safely be used.

Research uses the installed world maps, native scripts and read-only Ghidra
analysis. Automated checks cover map transitions, control ownership, changed
bindings, destination availability, submarine clearance and boarding contact.
Live gameplay and keyboard/SDL controller delivery still need player feedback.
Emerald tracking and avoidance continue to require positive on-screen visibility.
