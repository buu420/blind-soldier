# Blind Soldier 0.8.6

This beta adds destination navigation for the ordinary world-map submarine on
legacy x86/7th Heaven and native Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

- Use the existing navigation menu: R3 opens it, choose a destination, then
  A/X starts automatic travel. Keyboard controls remain J/L to select, I for
  spoken navigation, P for auto walk and K to repeat.
- Underwater Locations includes the Sunken Gelnika, Key of the Ancients and
  red submarine wreck when available. Steering uses the configured world-map
  turn and thrust controls, with corrected native underwater coordinates.
- Surface travel uses native directional controls and accounts for sideways
  movement while turning. Routes stay on submarine water and avoid unselected
  entrances. Getting off at a port remains manual; a destination without a water
  route is reported as unreachable from the submarine.
- Ordinary automatic travel rises to the submarine's entry depth and stays
  there, avoiding Emerald Weapon without tracking its hidden position. Above
  the destination, auto walk stops and asks you to hold Up to descend manually.
  The destination stays selected until its actual arrival condition is met.
  Leave depth controls alone during auto travel. Emerald can be encountered
  during the final descent or if you dive manually during travel.
- Emerald is announced and appears in Events only when the current rendered
  view confirms it is on screen, within the fog range and clear of terrain.
  Selecting Emerald explicitly permits automatic descent and approach. If its
  visibility cannot be confirmed, input pauses immediately. A sustained loss
  stops the approach with an explanation, while brief renderer gaps keep the
  selection without repeated sighting announcements.
- A deliberate Emerald approach also stops if diving or moving could enter
  the Sunken Gelnika, Key of the Ancients or a present red wreck first. It
  explains the obstruction so you can choose another approach.
- Legacy FFNx settings that combine external world meshes with analogue
  controls replace the submarine's controls. Auto travel explains that this
  combination needs to be disabled and the game restarted; spoken navigation
  remains available. Standard FFNx settings and native Steam x64 are supported.
- Pause, menus, dialogue, battles, lost focus, unreadable state and failed
  input delivery release automatic controls. Conflicting remapped controls
  are refused with guidance to remap them.

This uses normal game inputs. Submarine mission pursuit and firing guidance
from 0.8.5 retain their controls; firing remains manual.

Native research and automated checks cover map geometry, controls, visibility,
arrival conditions and routes against the installed surface and underwater maps.
Live game steering, actual Emerald encounters and controller hardware still need
gameplay feedback on both runtimes.
