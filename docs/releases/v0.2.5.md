# Blind Soldier 0.2.5 (Beta)

This beta release extends accessible progression from the world-map arrival
through Lower Junon and repairs several field and world-map navigation states
reported during live play.

## What changed

- Adds native Story routes for the remaining world-map progression windows,
  while exposing only destinations available at the current story state.
- Preserves active world-map navigation through battle and the native
  post-battle results module, then resumes the same route afterward.
- Adds Kalm and Chocobo Ranch Story targets, NPCs, objects, exits, and native
  route coverage.
- Adds concise audio descriptions for verified visual story beats from Kalm
  through Lower Junon.
- Uses the battle engine's active-enemy mask when announcing encounter size,
  preventing inactive actor records from inflating the enemy count.
- Repairs the post-collapse Aeris-house sequence and the Floor 63 coupon and
  duct objectives so Story follows the native progression state.
- Prevents route selection from claiming a distant target is already reached
  when the initial native waypoint is under the player.
- Keeps interaction arrival paused by measuring arrival and resume hysteresis
  against the same planned route, eliminating rapid reached/resumed speech.
- Keeps Jessie and Biggs available as Reactor 1 escape objectives until their
  native door-completion bits are set, even if the escape's game moment has
  advanced beyond the earlier catalog window.
- Applies the shared navigation and battle-state behavior to both the legacy
  2013 x86 and Steam 2026 x64 runtimes.

## Testing requested

On the world map, start a route, enter and finish a battle, and confirm the
route resumes without repeatedly announcing an update. In Kalm and at Chocobo
Ranch, confirm Story, NPC, Object, and Exit targets match what is currently
available.

During the Reactor 1 escape, return through the elevator and confirm Story
continues to Jessie and Biggs until both security doors are open. At an
interaction target reached through a trigger-line route, confirm Blind Soldier
announces arrival once and remains quiet until the player genuinely
backtracks.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching beta-channel update directly.
