# Blind Soldier 0.8.7

This beta adds an approach to your parked submarine under Transportation on
legacy x86/7th Heaven and native Steam x64. Close the game before updating.

- Select Submarine in the existing navigation menu to track its current parked
  position. Walking guidance ends where the game's own contact permits boarding.
- From the Highwind, navigation chooses grass where the ship can land and the
  party can walk to the submarine. At rest, it asks you to press Cancel to land.
  The selected route continues on foot; press Confirm to board the submarine.
- Flying over the boat is not announced as arrival. Boarding or losing the
  parked boat stops its route and releases automatic movement.
- A boat at a dock isolated from grass remains listed. Navigation explains
  when no Highwind landing can lead there on foot. Hidden and currently ridden
  submarines are excluded; an unboardable boat is reported without a route.

Use the usual R3 navigation menu and A/X for automatic travel. Keyboard controls
remain J/L to choose, I for spoken guidance, P for auto walk and K to repeat.

Research and automated checks cover native boarding contact, the installed dock
geometry, grass landing and walking transitions on both runtimes. Live gameplay,
controller hardware and actual hook installation still need player feedback.
