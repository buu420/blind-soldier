# Blind Soldier 0.5.11

This prerelease restores missing navigation targets in Gongaga Village on both supported game versions.

- The way out of the village appears in Exits during the early optional visit as well as later visits. It identifies the native Confirm interaction.
- Aerith and Tifa appear as navigation targets while their conversations after visiting Zack's parents are available. Navigation leads to their actual interaction areas. Hidden characters and completed conversations remain excluded.

The correction preserves the game's event conditions and choices. It does not automatically confirm conversations or change party or story flags. The voice-overlap fix from 0.5.10 is included; recorded descriptions are unchanged.

Validation uses the installed game's scripts, native interaction-state checks, and walkmesh approaches. The reporting player still needs to retest the in-game sequence after updating.

- **Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip:** both supported game runtimes.
- **Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip:** legacy 2013/7th Heaven x86.
- **ffviinew-v0.5.11-amm.zip** and **ffviiold-v0.5.11-amm.zip:** Accessibility Mod Manager packages.

Each archive has a SHA-256 sidecar. Preserve `Reloaded-II\Mods\ff7.accessibility.reloaded\Configuration\config.json` when updating a portable installation. Restart the game after updating.
