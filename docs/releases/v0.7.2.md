# Blind Soldier 0.7.2

This prerelease is for legacy x86/7th Heaven and Steam 2026 x64. It replaces the Temple of the Ancients clock room's long, queued descriptions with brief clock-time speech.

- **Brief clock time:** while the Time Guardian's hands move, the clock says "Moving, ten thirty." At most one update is spoken every 1.5 seconds. When a hand is between two numerals, the time is prefixed with "about", as in "Moving, about ten thirty." Once the hands come to rest, it says "Stopped, ten thirty." The short hand gives the hour and the long hand gives the minutes (its numeral times five). These are the hand positions on screen, not a prediction of where a spin will stop.
- **Stopped means stopped:** the clock says "Stopped" only after both hands stay still for 350 ms. It does not guess from a single look, so a hand passing a numeral during a spin is not reported as stopped. After entering the room, returning to the game or a failed read, the first time is heard once the hands have been watched briefly.
- **No stale queue:** each clock reading replaces the previous one instead of waiting behind it. Automatic clock speech no longer includes the room, bridge and second-hand details.
- **R gives the full detail:** it gives the time, the party's position in the room, all three hands and which bridges are open, using the game's own bridge state. Asking for it does not affect the automatic readings.
- **Other speech is protected:** clock readings wait until the Guardian's text, other dialogue and R details have finished being spoken. If the screen reader cannot report when it has finished, the mod waits for an estimated time based on the length of the text. A screen reader that never reports finishing delays the clock only for a limited time. The persistent "[OK] Stop!" window does not silence the clock after its words have been spoken.
- **Focus and mute:** clock speech pauses when the game is not in the foreground or speech is turned off. When it resumes, the current time is spoken once; readings missed in the meantime are not played.
- **Story hint:** Speed up time and Go back in time move the long hand one numeral, or five minutes.

## Time Guardian controls

The game's native controls are unchanged:

- At the Guardian's menu, MENU chooses Move it myself, OK chooses Spin and CANCEL chooses Proceed now!
- In Move it myself, OK (Speed up time) advances the long hand one numeral and MENU (Go back in time) moves it back. Passing twelve moves the short hand one hour. CANCEL chooses Proceed now!
- During a spin, the short hand runs backwards. OK slows the hands to a stop.
- The moving second hand is a separate hazard that can knock the party off a bridge. Avoiding it still depends on the player's timing.

## Testing

Both complete runtime suites, and the shared and parity suites, passed against the packaged DLLs. The tests replay the game's own hand timing through both runtimes' speech paths.

Live testing is needed. The clock room has not been played through with this build. Tester feedback on the pace and wording of the clock readings, and on whether they ever cut off the Guardian's text, is especially useful.
