# Blind Soldier 0.9.4

This beta repairs chocobo destination navigation in both the legacy x86/7th
Heaven and Steam 2026 x64 runtimes. Close the game before updating.

- Destination pathfinding uses the mounted bird's native terrain abilities.
  Green, blue, black and gold birds keep their actual mountain/water access;
  an ordinary bird is not given those abilities.
- A gold chocobo can navigate from water to the Knights of the Round, Mime,
  HP<->MP and Quadra Magic Materia caves and other reachable locations.
- Navigation holds at each Materia cave entrance and asks you to dismount.
  Dismounting and entering use the game's normal controls.
- Switching birds updates routes, reachable regions and entrance cues.
  Unreadable or changing native capability data cannot grant extra terrain access.
- Mounted steering uses the native chocobo movement step and contact footprint,
  so ordinary and colored birds can follow tight passes and bridges to Wutai.
- Emerald Weapon retains the native camera repair from 0.9.2. Select it in
  Events while visible to track and approach it; ordinary submarine journeys
  continue avoiding it. New logs record underwater visibility rejection reasons
  and identify the actual loaded mod build.

Use the existing navigation controls. Settings, saves, audio descriptions and
description history are preserved. This release does not start encounters or
operate dismount/entry controls automatically.

The supplied cumulative log's underwater visits predate the published 0.9.2
repair. They do not establish another failure in its current visibility reader.
Automated checks exercise native Emerald selection, patrol tracking, visibility
loss and avoidance, plus complete gold-chocobo cave and all-color Wutai journeys
with native terrain and contact-footprint replay at several camera angles in
both test hosts.
Live gameplay, hardware-controller delivery and speech listening remain
unverified for this repair. See the
[native validation evidence](../validation/chocobo-emerald-20261011.md).
