# Blind Soldier 0.3.1 (Beta)

This beta restores native menu speech on the Steam 2026 x64 world map.

## What changed

- Reads ordinary main-menu submenus such as Item, Magic, Materia, Equip,
  Status, Order, Limit, Config, PHS, Save, and Quit when they are opened while
  the player is on the world map.
- Uses the game's exact active menu-widget identity before granting speech
  ownership, so unrelated world-map overlays do not get mistaken for menus.
- Preserves the existing strict Save-menu handling and expires provisional
  menu evidence quickly when no verified menu widget becomes active.

## Testing requested

On Steam 2026 x64, stand on the world map, open the main menu, and check every
available submenu. Confirm that its rows, choices, and confirmations speak and
that normal world-map navigation remains available after closing the menu.

## Installation

Two ZIPs are available:

- `Blind-Soldier-Portable.zip` is the complete dual-runtime package for Steam
  2026 x64 or installations that need both x86 and x64 support.
- `Blind-Soldier-2013-x86-Portable.zip` is the smaller package for direct 2013
  x86 or stock 7th Heaven/FFNx.

Close Final Fantasy VII and 7th Heaven, then extract the appropriate ZIP over
the existing Blind Soldier installation. Accessibility Mod Manager users can
install the matching prerelease-channel update directly.
