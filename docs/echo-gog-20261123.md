# Echo-S dialogue, description reset and GOG support

Version 1.0.1 adds a saved **Read voiced dialogue** switch under
Speech in F11 settings. It starts off. FFNx's successful native `play_voice`
result suppresses the corresponding field window, including the optional raw
draw fallback. Unvoiced windows, missing voice files and questions retain their
speech. Unknown FFNx builds retain readable text rather than guessing playback.

The Downloads log records FFNx module SHA-256
`9C9859B1F66EE3A25D29BD5FC43171A3C298F4372A6AEE589808E8EC7431C77B`
being rejected by the old voice-hook allowlist. The official FFNx 1.25.2 release
contains that exact AF3DN.P. Its matching PDB names
`?play_voice@@YA_NPADEEE@Z` at RVA `0x00442C50`: a cdecl Boolean return with
`char*, byte, byte, byte` arguments. Ghidra confirms its non-relocated prefix
`55 8B EC 81 EC 0C 01 00 00`. The earlier 1.24.3 profile remains supported at
its own RVA; unknown hashes and modified prefixes remain rejected.

The matching voice-completion profile is also updated. PDB globals are
nxAudioEngine `0x027AA270` and current_opcode_message_status `0x02839900`;
Ghidra places the voice-slot map at nxAudioEngine+`0x70E30`, RVA `0x0281B0A0`.
The new isVoicePlaying, isValidVoiceHandle and getPause cohort is verified at
`0x00308280`, `0x019577D0` and `0x01957350`. Ordinary handle generations,
voice pointers at Soloud+`0x50`, and pause flag 4 are unchanged. Paused, unknown,
replaced and unreadable states still cannot authorize the creature narration.

Echo-S's leading not-equal glyph is removed at the Prism output boundary only from a speaker/name followed
by quoted dialogue. Genuine mathematical text retains the symbol.

The second F11 entry remains **Reset battle descriptions for this save**; it
already resets limit breaks, summons and summoning introductions. The third
entry, **Reset all descriptions for this save**, also resets room arrivals.
Activate either twice. Other saves keep their histories. Narration already
playing may finish, but cannot mark itself heard after the reset. Arrivals
replay on the next visit and battle descriptions on the next animation.
Films and other scene actions already replay at their next occurrence.

The missing Honeybee Inn lobby arrival is field 218, `onna_4`. Its scenery-only
cue uses observation rather than a script offset, so changed Echo-S scripts do
not suppress it. Audio descriptions generated with
[ViddyScribe](https://viddyscribe.com); the draft was reviewed against native
artwork, edited and recorded in Brice's previously approved own voice.

The supplied `Blind-Soldier-Portable-GOG.zip` has SHA-256
`E840A12A549AB6EF56F37CC7112D61718015F99DAA60D4D796DEB9B7E0E490EE`.
Its 1.0.0-gog.1 contribution supplies GOG executable fingerprint
`458539466A971406C5BFA2D02645DE7E5D6DC536463BC6434ECF9EDAB6C527A6`.
That exact identity is incorporated in the source/native allowlists; executable
architecture, callback signatures, resolver signatures and ownership checks
remain required. Movie callback proofs retain the actual edition's hash.
GOG save discovery and the accessible launcher use
`FINAL FANTASY VII GOG Edition` separately from Steam's directory. The launcher
recognizes the contribution's `goggame-1698970154.info` marker.

No GOG game executable or local GOG installation was supplied. Its launch and
live save/menu behavior therefore need GOG gameplay validation; a synthetic
fingerprint test does not establish those. No third-party rebuilt dependencies
from the ZIP replace the project's existing dependencies. The corrected stock
7th Heaven/ReShade loader layout is retained, with no executable `.local` folder.
