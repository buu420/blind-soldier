Battle animation descriptions, revision 2, 2026-09-28

93 animation identities, described in 175 recorded visual cues: 18 summon
variants, 49 regular Limit Breaks, 12 Vincent transformations/attacks, and
14 Cait Sith outcomes. Long animations have several cues describing appearance,
movement, and the attack as those stages occur. Short chain moves stay concise.
The wording concerns visible actions rather than hidden statistics or promised
combat results. Source footage is not distributed.

Visual references:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0t8yQ47qvQI (summons)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hITXcwHMEJA (Limit Breaks and variants)

Scripts and timing were reviewed directly against extracted reference frames,
including half-second samples at action starts and complex transitions.
Descript was offered these reference videos for a second visual review but did
not complete that review. It did not produce these scripts or voice recordings.

Cue offsets use vanilla 15-Hz battle ticks. Limits anchor to their native action
row; summons anchor to the native summon dispatcher handing off to the summon
sequence, after casting. FFNx 30/60-fps battle modes scale the authored ticks.
Native timing/anchor addresses were checked in both executables using Ghidra.
Visual offsets are reference-footage estimates, not frame-exact live calibration.
Final synchronization in both running games still requires play testing.

Narration uses Brice's approved reference recording and local Qwen3-TTS,
with his permission to synthesize and distribute his voice in this mod.
No friend's voice, reference sample, model weights, or private recordings are
included. English, temperature 0.68, top_p 0.9; natural generated timing,
without tempo or pitch manipulation. Loudness is matched to the existing voice
assets, with clipping checked after Vorbis decode.

Each recording received an independent local spoken-word transcription check.
Flagged results received a second pass with voice-activity detection; ambiguous
or clipped takes were replaced and checked again. Expected name spellings and
homophones are reviewed separately from actual added or missing words.
Recording lengths were checked against the ordered cue timings, including
Tifa's complete chain and Vincent's transformation followed by an attack.

Revision 2 has its own once-per-save history identities, so a save that heard
the earlier brief descriptions can hear this expanded set once. Existing movie
and field recordings, configuration, and save history are preserved.

Summoning-opening addition, 2026-09-28:
One additional own-voice cue describes the shared colored-orb and green-light
casting effect. It plays once per save, after the native attack-name banner
appears and its screen-reader speech has finished. The displayed attack name
comes from the game's loaded text and is announced on each cast. The existing
175 main cues and their timing are unchanged. The new clip is 2.92 seconds,
at natural speed, and passed the same decoded-audio and exact-word checks.
